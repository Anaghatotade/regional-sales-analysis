# Regional Sales Analysis 

This project focuses on analyzing and visualizing regional sales performance using Python, Excel, and Power BI. The workflow involves performing exploratory data analysis (EDA) for data understanding and cleaning, followed by building an interactive Power BI dashboard to derive actionable business insights.

## 🎯 Objective

To explore and visualize regional sales data to:
1. Understand revenue and profit distribution across states and regions
2. Detect high and low-performing areas
3. Identify top-selling categories and products
4. Support business strategy through actionable insights

## ⚙️ Tools & Technologies
| Tool                                            | Purpose                                         |
| ----------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- |
| **Python (Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn)** | Data Cleaning & Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) |
| **Excel**                                       | Initial data understanding and preprocessing    |
| **Power BI**                                    | Dashboard creation and data visualization       |

1. Top Products: Product 26 (117M) & Product 25 (109M) lead sales.
2. Most Efficient: Product 9 & 30 achieve the highest 40% profit margin.
3. Revenue Sources:
        Wholesale – 54%

5. Balanced margin (~37–38%) across all channels indicates consistent pricing strategy.

### ---> Regional Insights

1. California leads with 19.5% of total revenue ($228.8M).
2. West Region records the highest profit margin (37.5%).
3. Revenue Distribution:
    Midwest – 30.1% | South – 27.1% | Northeast – 25.9% | West – 16.9%
4. Profitability steady (~37%) across all regions.
5. Top-performing states: CA, TX, NY, FL.
6. Low-performing states: WY, SD, DE — potential growth zones.
